Franchise Opportunity Profile

Glo Tanning

A luxury sun spa franchise offering tanning and spa services. Every fact on this page is sourced from the GLO Tanning Franchise, LLC 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued June 16, 2025, as amended September 15, 2025), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$45,000

Uniformly charged to all franchisees. Additional units: greater of $30,000 or $15,000 off the then-current fee. Non-refundable. (FDD Item 5, p.11)

Estimated Initial Investment

$757,200 to $1,478,167

$97,500 to $102,500 must be paid to the franchisor or affiliates. Real estate improvements $258,000-$565,000; equipment/furniture/fixtures $315,000-$550,000. (FDD Item 7, p.17)

Royalty

6.5% of Gross Sales

Payable monthly, received by the 10th of the following month. Gross sales exclude sales tax. (FDD Item 6, p.12)

Marketing Fund Fee

3% of Gross Sales

Payable monthly. Used for national/regional marketing and brand development at the franchisor's discretion. (FDD Item 6, p.12)

Grand Opening Advertising

$50,000

Lump sum upon signing, paid to us and affiliates. (FDD Item 7, p.17)

Territory

Exclusive

No other GLO Tanning unit within your territory while in strict compliance. Usually a 2-mile radius; mall/facility sites bounded by the facility. (FDD Item 12, p.31)

Term

10 years + 10-year successor

No right to renew; if in good standing you may enter a successor agreement for 10 more years ($10,000 successor fee). (FDD Item 17, p.37)

Opening Assistance

$2,500-$7,500, up to 5 days

Up to 3 franchisor representatives on site for grand opening; franchisor conducts all grand-opening marketing. (FDD Item 11, p.24)

Time to Open

6 to 9 months

Must begin operations no later than 12 months from signing; 60 days to select site; lease in place within 6 months. (FDD Item 11, pp.24-25)

Financing

Not offered

The franchisor does not offer direct or indirect financing and does not guarantee your obligations. (FDD Item 10, p.24)

Tan Tax

10% federal excise tax

Clients are assessed 10% federal excise tax on all UV tanning services under PPACA; you collect and remit it. (FDD Item 1, p.10)

HQ

Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

12335 N. Rockwell Ave., Oklahoma City, OK 73142. (405) 708-6320. (FDD cover; FDD Item 1, p.9)

System Size

82 outlets (15 states)

77 franchised + 5 company-owned at 12/31/2024; up from 40 total at end of 2022. Zero terminations, reacquisitions, or ceased operations 2022-2024. (FDD Item 20, p.45)

Litigation / Bankruptcy

None disclosed

No litigation or bankruptcy is required to be disclosed in Items 3 or 4. (FDD Items 3-4, p.11)

Risk Profile & Franchise Considerations

45 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate. A fast-growing, litigation-free system with an exclusive-territory model, tempered by a capital-intensive build-out and a concentrated seasonal demand cycle.

Risk inputs (published weights):

  • Base: 50
  • Capital intensity: +10 (investment ceiling $1,478,167)
  • Fee burden: +5 (royalty 6.5% + marketing fund 3% = 9.5% of gross sales)
  • Seasonality: +5 (majority of annual sales February–June)
  • No litigation disclosed: −10
  • Zero terminations, reacquisitions, or ceased operations 2022–2024: −10
  • Strong unit growth: −5 (+32 net franchised outlets in 2024)

Method: 50 + 10 + 5 + 5 − 10 − 10 − 5 = 45. Published so an independent verifier can recompute it. A franchise offer can only be made through a Franchise Disclosure Document.
